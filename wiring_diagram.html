🦜 ANIMATRONIC BIRD SHOW HARDWARE SCHEMATIC

Complete Master Wiring Map for Raspberry Pi, PCA9685 PWM Driver, L298N Motors & Relays

🍓 Raspberry Pi MCU

Controls Web server, Gemini Voice AI, I2C bus (Pin 3 SDA, Pin 5 SCL), L298N drivers & Chirp Relays.

🎛️ PCA9685 16-Ch PWM

I2C Address 0x40. Drives all 16 stage servos (3-DOF perches, 2-DOF spotlights, turntable base).

⚙️ Dual L298N H-Bridges

Parrot 1 & Parrot 2 Mouth Solenoids (Ch A), Blinking Eyes (Reverse Polarity), Flapping Wings (Ch B).

🐥 Relays & Power Rail

Relay #1 (GPIO 17) & Relay #2 (GPIO 27). High-Current 12A Power Supply with Shared Common Ground Bus.
