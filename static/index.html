⚠️ HARDWARE ASSEMBLY & POWER SAFETY INSTRUCTIONS

Important Specs

1. Common Ground Connection: You MUST connect the GND terminal of the Raspberry Pi, PCA9685, both L298N motor drivers, the relay module, and the external power supply together to a shared common ground bus.

2. High-Current Servo Power (V+): Do NOT power the servos directly from the Raspberry Pi 5V pins! Use a dedicated 5V or 6V DC power supply capable of supplying 10A - 12A to the PCA9685 screw terminal (V+ and GND).

3. L298N Motor Driver Jumpers: Ensure the 5V enable jumpers on both L298N boards are kept attached if input voltage is 12V or lower, or power logic via Pi 5V rail.

4. Wire Color Code: Red = +5V VCC, Black/Grey = Ground (GND), Yellow/Cyan = PWM / Servo Signals, Magenta/Purple = L298 Motor Control, Orange/Gold = Relay Triggers.
